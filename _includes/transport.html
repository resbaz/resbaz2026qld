Resbaz will be at QUT Kelvin Grove campus. (See QUT PDF map for this campus.)

Getting to campus

QUT Kelvin Grove is accessible by public transport and cycling (see a map of cycle paths on OSM). We encourage to opt for one of these more sustainable options, but if you need to drive, see the important information about parking below.

Public transport

Public transport is currently 50 cents per trip, making it a lot more affordable than driving! The most convenient public transport stop to get to ResBaz is the QUT Kelvin Grove station, 400 m from the campus. Plan your public transport journey with Translink's Journey Planner. From the centre of Brisbane, this is a direct trip taking between 10 and 15 minutes.

More information about transport can be found on the QUT website.

On campus

On Day 1, head to the registration desk outside the F Block Lecture Theatre (Room 509, Level 5, F Block, Kelvin Grove campus).
After the Welcome Address, and for the rest of the conference, you will find the registration desk at the E5 Education Precinct Function Room (Level 5, E Block, Kelvin Grove campus). Wayfinding signs will be placed around campus to help attendees to find rooms.
For more information about accessibility, please see the Kelvin Grove campus Accessibility Map.
